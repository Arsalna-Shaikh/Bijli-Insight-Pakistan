Why this matters

Utilities, businesses and households plan load and budgets around short-range heat. Bijli-Insight turns open weather archives and published NEPRA tariffs into forecasts with an honest range, so planners prepare for the worst case, not only the average, and every number can be traced back to a fingerprinted source file.
